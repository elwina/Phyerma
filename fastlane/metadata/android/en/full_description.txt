Phyerma turns the phone you are holding into a physics lab.

Run a ready-made experiment, or open the sensors directly: acceleration, rotation, magnetic field, light, pressure, sound, location, and camera. Export the data as CSV, TSV, or Excel. Turn on remote access and drive the experiment from a browser on the same network — nothing to install on the computer.

What you can see about this phone:

• Brand, model, and system skin (HyperOS, ColorOS, OriginOS, MagicOS, HarmonyOS, and others).
• A match against a crowd-sourced sensor database, shown as a baseline. It is a reference. It is not applied as a hidden correction.
• A sensor lab: SI units, measured noise, measured sample rate, and the range Android claims, labeled as a claim.
• Chinese and English.

Sensor readings stay on the phone unless you export them, enable remote access, or start an experiment that itself uses the network. Phyerma does not use Google Play Services, Firebase, Crashlytics, or an advertising SDK.

Phyerma is an open-source fork of phyphox-android (GPL-3.0). The names “phyphox” and “RWTH Aachen University” are trademarks of the upstream project. Phyerma is not published by RWTH Aachen.

Source: https://github.com/elwina/Phyerma

Sensors (not every phone has every one): accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, light, pressure, proximity, microphone, GPS, camera, depth.
